Getting started

What is Tranwrecks Studio?

A web-based marketing planning workspace with a guided Campaign Builder, suggested marketing tactics, a digital marketing playbook, analytics calculator and content calendar.

How do I start a campaign?

Select Campaign Builder from the website, review a membership, and choose the clearly labeled free preview while billing is being set up. Answer the nine questions in order to receive a draft blueprint.

Is the marketing content generated by a live AI model?

Not currently. The campaign and calendar previews use rule-based templates and existing tactics. Verify factual claims, marketing assumptions and budgets before publication.

Campaigns & tactics

How does Smart Recommendations work?

It ranks tactics using your marketing goal and selected channel, then shows a shortlist. These are planning recommendations, not predictions of campaign success.

How do I save a recommended tactic?

Open Smart Recommendations, select your goal and channel, and choose a tactic. The selection is carried into your browser's campaign draft.

Can I export my campaign?

Yes. The Campaign Builder offers copy and downloadable text options after completing the guided questions.

How many monthly campaigns are in each plan?

Starter: 3; Pro: 15; Super Pro Plus: 50. These are proposed subscription allowances, not yet enforced because secure paid access is not active.

Plans & payments

How much does membership cost?

Displayed proposed monthly prices are Starter $4.99, Pro $9.99 and Super Pro Plus $15.99. View the membership page for the latest displayed plan details.

Can I pay and get instant access today?

Not yet. The Stripe-hosted subscription checkout, account login, and automatic entitlement verification are not deployed and tested. The current checkout page does not charge you.

When will paid access activate?

After the account backend, subscription products, webhook verification and member-only access are deployed and tested. No launch date is currently published.

Where will I manage my subscription?

The planned account dashboard will direct subscribers to Stripe's Customer Portal for supported subscription changes once billing is activated. Until then no paid subscription can be managed through this website.

Digital resources

Is the digital book included?

Yes. The ten-chapter Modern Marketing Playbook is included in all proposed plans. A development preview is currently accessible; paid-only access is not yet enforced.

Is the Content Calendar Generator included?

Yes. All three plans include the 30-Day Content Calendar Generator, which creates a draft posting schedule based on your inputs. It offers CSV export but does not publish to social media.

How do I save the book as a PDF?

Open the playbook preview, then use its Print / Save as PDF control and select your browser's PDF printer.

Troubleshooting

The site looks outdated or a button doesn't work.

Refresh the page with Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac. If the problem persists, try an incognito window and confirm the address is tranwrecks.com.

My answers or saved tactics disappeared.

Current previews store some choices in the browser's local or session storage. Clearing browser data, using a different device, or ending a session can affect those drafts. Cloud account synchronization is not currently available.

The website says Under Construction.

This can indicate GitHub Pages or domain routing is not serving the correct site. Check the Pages deployment status and domain configuration rather than entering payment details on a placeholder website.
